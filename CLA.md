# OpenEMT Individual Contributor Licence Agreement

*Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor License
Agreement v2.0.*

Thank you for contributing to OpenEMT. This agreement clarifies the
intellectual property licence granted with Contributions from any person or
entity. It protects you as much as it protects the project: it records exactly
what you are granting, and confirms that you had the right to grant it.

**You keep the copyright in your Contribution.** You are granting a licence,
not giving anything away. You remain free to use your own work anywhere else,
for anything, forever.

## Why this is required

OpenEMT is dual licensed: AGPL-3.0-only for everyone, plus a commercial
licence for those who cannot use the AGPL (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)).
Offering a commercial licence requires the Maintainer to hold rights broad
enough to grant it across the whole work. A single contribution received
without those rights would carve a permanent hole in that ability, and would
equally block any future move to a different licence. Collecting agreements
afterwards does not work, because one unreachable past contributor is enough
to make it impossible.

## How to sign

Add a line to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) in your first pull request:

```
Full Name <email> — I agree to the OpenEMT Individual CLA (CLA.md), YYYY-MM-DD.
```

Signing with your pull request is sufficient. The commit is the record: it is
signed by your Git identity, timestamped, and permanent.

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer and need a corporate
agreement instead, contact **licensing@openemt.pro**.
