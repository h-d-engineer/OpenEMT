# Trademark and naming policy

The AGPL grants rights in the **code**. It grants no rights in the **name**.
This page says what you may and may not do with "OpenEMT".

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your work uses, is built on, is compatible with, or interoperates
  with OpenEMT.
- Redistribute unmodified OpenEMT under its own name.
- Write about it, teach with it, benchmark it, publish comparisons, and
  criticise it. Accurate statements about the software are always fine.
- Cite it in academic work. See [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) for the preferred
  form.

## What needs a different name

If you distribute a **modified** version, please do not present it as
"OpenEMT" without qualification. Call it something of your own, and describe
it as based on or derived from OpenEMT.

This is not about control over your fork, which the AGPL guarantees. It is
about a user being able to tell whose engineering results they are looking at.
An EMT simulator's output gets used to size equipment and set protection. If a
modified build produces a different answer, users need to know which build
produced it, and the name is how they know.

## What is not permitted

- Using the name, or a confusingly similar one, in a way that suggests your
  product, service, or company is official, endorsed, affiliated, or
  maintained by the OpenEMT project when it is not.
- Using it in your own product name, company name, domain, or logo in a way
  likely to cause that confusion.
- Presenting a modified build's results as OpenEMT's results.

## Related naming note

OpenEMT is **not** EMTP, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived
from EMTP or the EMTP Alliance. "EMTP" is their mark. OpenEMT is an
independently written EMT simulator that implements classical, published
methods (Dommel's trapezoidal nodal formulation and its descendants). Please
do not describe OpenEMT as "an EMTP".

Similarly, OpenEMT is unaffiliated with PSCAD, PSS/E, PowerFactory, ATP,
OpenDSS, or any other simulator it can exchange data with or is compared
against. Those names belong to their respective owners and are used here only
to describe interoperability and validation references factually.

## Asking

For anything this page does not clearly permit, or if you think your case is
reasonable and it falls on the wrong side of a line above, just ask:
**licensing@openemt.pro**. The intent is to prevent user confusion, not to stop
people building things.

## Honest scope note

"OpenEMT" is a largely descriptive name, which limits how much exclusivity it
can carry as a matter of trademark law regardless of what this policy says.
This policy therefore describes the project's expectations and asks for good
faith. It is not a claim to a broad monopoly on the words.
